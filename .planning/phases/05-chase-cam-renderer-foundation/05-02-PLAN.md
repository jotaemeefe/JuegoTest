<objective>
Build the core deliverable of Phase 5: the scanline segment projector and chase camera that replace the top-down world blit. Every frame the projector walks SEGMENTS[] forward from the camera's z, folds curve and elevation into a single perspective projection per segment, and draws back-to-front trapezoids (road, kerb, rumble bands) sampling the reused pixel texture, capped at a fixed draw-distance. The camera derives its z and lateral offset from the player's existing world-space state (D-03) and frames the kart low and close (D-10).

Purpose: This is RENDER-01 itself and the correctness proof for RENDER-03 (curve+crest) and RENDER-04 (draw-distance culling). Per D-01 there is no separate harness — it is wired directly into the live loop so hitting play shows it.
Output: A working chase-cam road in game.js, replacing drawTrack/buildEnvCanvas/tunnel top-down rendering.
</objective>
