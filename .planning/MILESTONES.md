# Milestones

## v2.0 Colapinto F1 Racer (Shipped: 2026-08-15)

**Phases completed:** 8 phases (1, 2, 2b, 2c, 3, 3b, 4A, 4B), 21 plans

**Key accomplishments:**

- Monaco circuit built from scratch as a non-crossing 57-point spine (replacing the original oval), with a rotating/translate follow camera, minimap, and barrier walls that are the circuit (no run-off).
- Multi-car racing with personality-driven AI (aggressive/defensive/consistent), full pairwise collision detection, and a live P1-P4 HUD — later focused to a tight 1v1 duel with real racecraft: predictive traffic avoidance, defensive blocking, catch-up rubber-band, and visible pressure mistakes.
- Physics overhaul: exponential speed-approach model (fixed a terminal-velocity bug that made every speed cap decorative), wall grinding vs. crash penalty, and bump-and-run collisions that slide instead of sticking.
- Honest race feedback: real segment-crossing finish line, continuous track-progress ranking (replacing checkpoint-order flicker), real-seconds gaps, and overtake events that fire once per actual pass.
- Audio, VFX and DRS: synthesized engine/brake audio, progressive damage tint + impact shake, overtake flash, and a legal one-use-per-lap DRS mechanic shared by player and AI.
- R4A/R4B pixel-art evolution: 22-car five-lap Grand Prix (P12 player start), 135%-scale widened circuit for pack racing, a native 16-cell tile/palette pixel-art rendering pipeline, uniquely placed props/crowds, and a clean-racing mastery score.
